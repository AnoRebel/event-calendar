Event Calendar is two things:

- **A calendar component** you can copy into any Vue 3 or Nuxt project with the shadcn-vue CLI. It renders month,
  week, day and agenda views, handles drag and drop, resizing and overlapping events, and leaves your data to you.
- **A complete, self-hostable calendar app** built around that component: accounts with passkeys, per-user events
  stored in libSQL, optional real-time sync, an installable PWA, and these docs.

## Highlights

- **Four views.** Month, week, day and agenda, with keyboard shortcuts (`M`, `W`, `D`, `A`), swipe navigation on touch
  screens, and shareable URLs (`?view=week&date=2026-11-03`).
- **Direct manipulation.** Drag events between days and times, onto or off the all-day row, and resize them from either
  edge. It works with a mouse, a pen or a finger (press and hold), shows a live preview, and saves once on release.
- **Overlaps that stay readable.** Events that overlap sit side by side and re-flow while you drag.
- **Timezones.** Times are entered in any timezone and shown in the viewer's own.
- **Passkeys.** Create an account and sign in with a fingerprint, face or screen lock; passwords remain available.
- **Crystal Neuo.** Six materials, five platform styles, light, dark and high-contrast themes and three densities,
  switchable from the Appearance dialog and applied before the first paint.
- **Accessible and responsive.** Works from 360px wide upward, with native focus handling, touch-sized targets and
  reduced-motion and reduced-transparency support.

## Tech stack

Nuxt 4 (Vue 3, TypeScript) on Bun · shadcn-vue and Reka UI · Tailwind CSS v4 · Crystal Neuo · libSQL with Drizzle ORM ·
nuxt-auth-utils (sealed-cookie sessions, WebAuthn) · TanStack Query and TanStack Form with valibot · date-fns and
@date-fns/tz · VueUse · Nuxt Content.
